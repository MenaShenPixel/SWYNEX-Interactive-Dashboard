📊 Task 3: Interactive Sales Dashboard using Power BI

📌 Project Overview
As part of my SWYNEX Data Analytics Internship, I developed an interactive sales dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze business performance and communicate key findings through interactive visualizations.
Building on the previous tasks — Data Cleaning & Preparation and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) — this project uses the prepared Global Superstore dataset to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), explore sales and profitability, and compare business performance across markets, customer segments, and product categories.

🎯 Project Objectives
Develop an interactive dashboard to monitor business performance.
Track key sales and profitability KPIs.
Analyze sales and profit trends over time.
Compare performance across markets, customer segments, and product categories.
Enable dynamic data exploration through interactive filters.
Communicate business findings through clear and effective visualizations.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Power BI: Dashboard development and data visualization.
Power Query: Data transformation and preparation.
DAX: KPI calculations and business metrics.
Interactive Filters & Slicers: Dynamic data exploration.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard focuses on key business performance metrics, including:
Total Sales: Overall sales revenue.
Total Profit: Total profit generated.
Profit Margin: Profitability relative to sales.
Total Orders: Number of distinct orders.
Total Customers: Number of distinct customers.
Average Order Value (AOV): Average sales value per order.

📊 Dashboard Visualizations
The dashboard provides visual insights into different aspects of business performance, including:
Sales & Profit Trends: Analyze performance over time.
Sales & Profit margin by  Market: Compare sales contributions across markets.
Sales by Customer Segment: Understand the contribution of different customer segments.
Category Performance: Compare sales and profitability across product categories.
Market Profitability: Evaluate differences in profitability across markets.

🎛️ Interactive Filters
The dashboard uses interactive slicers to help users explore the data from different perspectives, including:
Year
Market
Category
Customer Segment
These filters allow users to dynamically adjust the displayed metrics and visualizations to investigate specific business areas.

🔍 Business Analysis
The dashboard helps users:
Identify sales and profit trends.
Compare performance across different markets.
Understand customer segment contributions to sales.
Evaluate product category performance.
Identify differences between sales volume and profitability.
Explore business metrics using interactive filters.

Task 3 – Interactive Dashboard: Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize KPIs and communicate key findings.
